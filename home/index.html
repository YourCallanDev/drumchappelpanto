Welcome to Drumchapel Amateur Panto Club!

Step off the yellow brick road and into a world of laughter, magic, and unforgettable performances! Based in the heart of Drumchapel, Glasgow, our amateur theatre club brings the community together through spectacular pantomimes, exciting productions, and fun-filled fundraising events. Whether you're a performer, backstage wizard, or audience member, there's a place for everyone in our magical theatre family… oh yes there is!
