About Us

OUR STORY

A community built on theatre and togetherness.

✦

Launched in 2022 by Maggie Harkness-Smith, Drumchapel Amateur Panto Club is more than just a theatre group — it’s a vibrant, welcoming community built on creativity, confidence, and connection.

From the very beginning, our goal has been simple: bring people together through the magic of pantomime.

Whether you're stepping onto the stage for the first time, working behind the scenes, or supporting from the audience, DAPC is a place where everyone belongs. We celebrate individuality, encourage growth, and create unforgettable experiences filled with laughter, music, and a touch of theatrical magic… oh yes we do!
