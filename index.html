About Us

OUR STORY

A community built on theatre and togetherness.

✦

Launched in 2022 by Maggie Harkness-Smith, Drumchapel Amateur Panto Club is more than just a theatre group — it’s a vibrant, welcoming community built on creativity, confidence, and connection.

From the very beginning, our goal has been simple: bring people together through the magic of pantomime.

Whether you're stepping onto the stage for the first time, working behind the scenes, or supporting from the audience, DAPC is a place where everyone belongs. We celebrate individuality, encourage growth, and create unforgettable experiences filled with laughter, music, and a touch of theatrical magic… oh yes we do!

EVERYONE DESERVES TO ENJOY THE MAGIC

Show Accessibility

SUPPORT · CONNECT · INCLUDE · EMPOWER

Making theatre accessible for everyone.

✦

Drumchapel Amateur Panto Group is proud to be working in partnership with Neurodivergent Family Circle to make our shows more accessible, welcoming and comfortable for everyone.

Our aim is to help audience members who may need additional support feel safe, included and able to enjoy the magic of live theatre.

♡

OUR ACCESSIBILITY PARTNER

Neurodivergent Family Circle

As the Inclusion & Support Coordinator for Neurodivergent Family Circle, my role is to help audience members who may need additional support when attending our shows.

Working together to make theatre more welcoming and accessible for everyone.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Accessibility Support List

To make sure we can provide the best possible support, your name must be added to our accessibility support list before the show.

This allows us to plan ahead, arrange appropriate seating and have sensory resources ready for you when you arrive.

♡

You don't need to explain or justify your needs. We're here to listen, understand and support.

We may ask a few simple questions to help make sure you receive the correct support.

♡
✉

NEED ACCESSIBILITY SUPPORT?

Get in touch with us

To be added to the accessibility list, ask a question or discuss any support you may need, please contact Neurodivergent Family Circle directly.

neurodivergentfamilycircle@gmail.com
