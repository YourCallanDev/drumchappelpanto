OUR STORY

Launched in 2022 by Maggie Harkness-Smith, Drumchapel Amateur Panto Club is more than just a theatre group — it’s a magical community where creativity, confidence, and friendship come to life. From first-time performers to experienced creatives, we welcome everyone to step into the spotlight or work their magic behind the scenes. Together, we bring laughter, joy, and unforgettable pantomime experiences to our local community… oh yes we do!
